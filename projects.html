4-bit ripple-carry adder

CourseworkDigital design

The adder takes two 4-bit numbers, a and b, plus a carry-in, and produces a 4-bit sum and a carry-out. Instead of writing one big block of logic, I wrote a single full-adder module and instantiated it four times. Each stage passes its carry-out to the next stage's carry-in, which is what makes it a ripple-carry design.

The full adder is written in dataflow style. XOR gates produce the sum bit, and AND and OR gates produce the carry. Opening the synthesized schematic was the best part: it shows exactly how the HDL I wrote maps onto real gates.

Tools
Vivado
Concepts
Hierarchical design, module reuse, combinational logic, carry propagation
Ports
a[3:0], b[3:0], cin in; s[3:0], cout out
